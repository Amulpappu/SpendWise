## 📱 About SpendWise

**SpendWise** is a personal finance app for Android that automatically tracks your bank SMS and UPI transactions in real-time. It runs completely offline on your device, calculates your live bank balances, exports password-protected bank statements, and syncs seamlessly with your Google Sheets — with **zero data leaks**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* ⚡ **100% Offline Bank SMS Auto-Detection**: Automatically reads debit and credit SMS alerts and calculates running balances instantly without internet.
* 📱 **Paytm-Style Interface**: Sleek executive dashboard with instant search by merchant, amount, category, or bank account, plus an in-app 24x7 Help & Support Center.
* 🔒 **Password-Protected Bank Statements**: Generate official PDF and Excel account statements stamped with the SpendWise brand logo and protected with your custom password formula (e.g. `LOHI5779`).
* 📊 **Multi-User Google Sheets Live Sync**: Each family member or user automatically gets their own dedicated ledger tab (e.g. `Lohith (2741)`) in your central spreadsheet.
* 🔐 **Offline SMS OTP Verification**: On-device verification with automatic SMS detection for instant, secure onboarding.
* 🛡️ **Zero Cloud Leaks**: Your banking and SMS data is stored locally on your phone and is never sent to external third-party servers.

## 🔒 Statement Password Formulas

Your exported PDF statements are protected with deterministic passwords:

* **Name + Account (Default)**: First 4 letters of your Name (CAPS) + Last 4 digits of your Account Number (e.g., `LOHI5779` or `NAVE4561`).
* **Name + Mobile**: First 4 letters of Name + Last 4 digits of Mobile (e.g., `LOHI2741`).
* **Name (End) + Mobile (Start)**: Last 4 letters of Name + First 4 digits of Mobile (e.g., `HITH6379`).
